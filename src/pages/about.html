Who it's for

A good fit, and an honest not-a-fit.

Usually a great fit

  • A few people to a few locations, and an owner who still feels the busywork.
  • The same jobs every week. Reports, inbox, payables and follow-ups, by hand.
  • Software you already pay for. Email, spreadsheets, accounting, POS or CRM.

Probably not a fit

  • You only want a website chatbot. Off-the-shelf tools handle that for less.
  • The work is different every time. Automation pays off on repetition.
  • You need a large enterprise rollout. We focus on small teams.
