Who it's for

A good fit, and an honest not-a-fit.

Usually a great fit

  • A few people to a few locations. Big enough to have real busywork, small enough that the owner still feels it.
  • Someone is the bottleneck. An owner or office manager who builds the reports, enters the invoices and answers every question.
  • The same jobs every week. Reports, inbox, payables, forms and follow-ups done the same way, by hand.
  • Software you already pay for. Email, spreadsheets, accounting and a POS or CRM that exports data.

Probably not a fit

  • You want a chatbot for your website and nothing else. There are cheaper off-the-shelf tools for that.
  • The work changes completely every time. Automation pays back on repetition.
  • You need a large enterprise rollout. We build for small teams, fast.
  • No one can spare an hour to review the first build. Every automation runs side by side with you before it goes live.

Security and privacy

Your data stays yours.

The first question most owners ask, answered up front.

01

Not used to train AI

We use business API access to AI models, where your data is not used to train them, never the free consumer chat apps.

02

Only the access each job needs

Each automation gets its own limited access to the inboxes, folders and exports it works on. Nothing more, and you can revoke it any time.

03

Humans approve money and messages

Payments and outgoing messages wait for a person until you decide otherwise. Exceptions are flagged, never guessed at.

04

Confidential from the first call

Happy to sign your NDA before the audit. Everything we build is documented: what it touches, where it runs and how to switch it off.
