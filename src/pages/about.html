Who it's for

A good fit, and an honest not-a-fit.

Usually a great fit

  • A few people to a few locations. Real busywork, and an owner who still feels it.
  • Someone is the bottleneck. An owner or office manager doing reports, invoices and every question.
  • The same jobs every week. Reports, inbox, payables and follow-ups, by hand.
  • Software you already pay for. Email, spreadsheets, accounting, POS or CRM.

Probably not a fit

  • You only want a website chatbot. Off-the-shelf tools do that cheaper.
  • The work changes every time. Automation pays back on repetition.
  • You need a large enterprise rollout. We build for small teams, fast.
  • No one can spare an hour to review the first build. Every automation runs beside you first.

Security and privacy

Your data stays yours.
