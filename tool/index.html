The implementation plan

See the whole plan before it runs.

Before anything is switched on, Archer lays out exactly how the automation will work. Change any line with another sentence, then approve it.

  • When it runs. Every Monday at 7 am.
  • What it does. Pulls last week's sales, totals them by day and compares them to the week before.
  • Where it works. Reads from the systems you already use. Nothing new to install.
  • What you get. One email with the summary and the full report attached.
  • Your sign-off. You choose which steps run on their own and which wait for your OK.
