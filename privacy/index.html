Privacy Policy.

What we collect when you visit this site or get in touch, why, who handles it, and how to ask for a copy or deletion. Short version: no cookies, no ad tracking, and we only use what you send us to reply to you.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Who we are

Biddle Systems LLC ("Biddle Systems", "we", "us") is an AI automation consultancy based in Southern California. We operate biddlesystems.com (the "site") and an SMS assistant service. This policy covers both. It applies to visitors in the United States and anywhere else the site is reached.

Questions about this policy go to support@biddlesystems.com.

What we collect

Nothing automatically beyond ordinary web hosting. This site has no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no session-replay or heat-map tools. We do not fingerprint your browser. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third-party font service.

We receive information only when you choose to send it:

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, business name if you give it, and your message. These are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms (see Service providers).
  • Email. Anything you send to a biddlesystems.com address, including attachments.
  • Audit call and client work. If we work together, the systems, documents and data you give us access to are covered by your written agreement with us and, if you ask, an NDA. That data is handled as described there and is never used to train AI models.
  • SMS program. If you text our number, we receive your mobile number, the content of your messages, and the date and time they were sent. When you opt in or out by text, we record which keyword you sent and when, so we can honor your choice and show compliance. See the SMS Terms.

Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record the IP address and browser details of visitors in server logs for security and operations. We do not have access to those logs beyond aggregate counts. GitHub's handling is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

One setting stored on your device. If you press "Pause animations" in the footer, the site stores a single preference in your browser's local storage so the choice sticks. It contains no personal information, is never sent to us, and is removed when you press "Resume animations" or clear your browser data.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and schedule the free audit call you asked for.
  • To scope, quote, build and support the automations you hire us for.
  • To operate the SMS assistant and send only the alerts you have asked for.
  • To honor opt-out requests and keep the record of consent that messaging regulations require.
  • To keep the site and our systems secure.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build marketing profiles, and we do not add you to a newsletter list because you filled in the form.

Service providers

We use a small number of third parties strictly to deliver the service. They process data on our behalf under their own terms, and we do not authorize them to use your information for their own marketing.

  • Web3Forms delivers contact-form submissions to our inbox.
  • GitHub hosts the site (GitHub Pages).
  • Twilio carries SMS and voice traffic for our phone number.
  • Anthropic and OpenAI provide the AI models behind the assistant through business API access, where message content is sent to the model to produce a reply and is not used to train the models.
  • Google and Microsoft provide our business email and documents.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, except the service providers above that are strictly necessary to deliver the messages themselves.

We do not send advertising by text, and we do not use your data to train AI models.

How long we keep it

  • Contact form and email. Kept in our inbox while the conversation is useful, and deleted on request.
  • Client data. Kept for the length of the engagement plus whatever the written agreement says, then returned or deleted.
  • SMS message history. Kept only as long as it is useful to operate the assistant, on systems we control.
  • SMS consent records. The fact that you opted in or out, and when, is kept for as long as required to demonstrate compliance with messaging regulations, even after you unsubscribe.

Security

Access to your information is limited to the people who need it to reply to you or do the work, which at Biddle Systems is the founder. Message and client data live on private infrastructure that is not published or publicly accessible. Each automation we build gets only the access it needs, and you can revoke it at any time. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send payment card numbers, government identification numbers or passwords by text, email or the contact form.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and where it came from.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete it, except for records we are required to keep (such as SMS consent records).
  • Stop any messages: reply STOP to any text, or email us.

Email support@biddlesystems.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We will confirm your identity using the address or number the information came from, and respond within 45 days. There is no charge, and we will not treat you differently for asking.

California residents. The California Consumer Privacy Act gives California residents the rights to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We honor these requests from anyone, whether or not the law technically applies to a business of our size. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Do Not Track. Because the site does not track visitors at all, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals change nothing: there is no tracking to turn off.

Children

The site and the SMS service are for businesses and adults. They are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

The site links to a few outside pages, such as GitHub's privacy statement. Those sites have their own policies, and this one does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. If we ever started collecting something new, this page would say so before it happened.
