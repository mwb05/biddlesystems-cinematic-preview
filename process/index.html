How it works

Weeks, not quarters.

No six-month project, no software rollout. One job, automated against your real systems and proven before we touch the next. That is the automation-services path; the courses and the tool start from the same free audit.

From first call to hours back.

  1. Day 1 01

    Free audit call

    45 minutes. We map where your team's hours go, job by job. No pitch.

  2. Day 2-5 02

    Your hours, on paper

    A short written plan: each repetitive job, what it costs, which to automate first, and whether we build it, your team learns it, or the tool does it.

  3. Week 1-3 03

    Build

    The first automation, built against your real inbox, exports and systems. Not a sandbox.

  4. Week 3-4 04

    Run it side by side

    Next to your current process until you trust the output. Then the manual version stops.

  5. Day 30 05

    Live

    The first job runs on its own. Those hours come back that week.

  6. Every month 06

    Run and add

    We watch it, fix what breaks, and add the next job when you're ready.

What you get

Clear terms. You own the result.

  • A fixed quote per build. Known before anything starts.
  • Your systems, not new ones. The email, spreadsheets and software you already use.
  • A human in the loop. Drafts and payments wait for approval.
  • Plain-English documentation. What it does and how to turn it off.
  • You own everything. Automations, data and docs.
  • Month to month. No long contract.

Questions

The ones everyone asks.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can tell us what your team does on a Monday morning, that is enough.

Which of the three services should I start with?

The audit answers that. Want the busywork gone? Automation services. Want your team to own it? The courses and the tool. They combine fine; the plan says which fits each job.

Is my data safe?

Each job gets only the access it needs, a person approves anything that sends money or messages, and we document where your data goes.

What if it gets something wrong?

Every build runs beside your current process first, and exceptions go to a person. When something breaks, monthly support covers the fix.

Will this replace my staff?

The busywork, not the people. Your team gets its week back for the work that needs them.

How does pricing work?

A fixed price per build, quoted after the audit and sized to the hours it saves. Then optional month-to-month support for monitoring, fixes and small changes. If the numbers don't work for you, we'll say so.

Which AI do you use?

Whichever fits the job, usually Claude or ChatGPT through business API access, where your data is not used to train the models.

What happens if we stop working together?

You keep everything. The automations run in your accounts and the documentation explains them. Nothing is held hostage.
