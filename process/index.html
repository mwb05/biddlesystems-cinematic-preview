How it works

Weeks, not quarters.

No six-month project, no software rollout, no retraining your team. We pick one job, automate it against your real systems, and prove it before we touch the next one.

From first call to hours back.

  1. Day 1 01

    Free audit call

    45 minutes. We map where your team's hours actually go, job by job. No deck, no pitch.

  2. Day 2-5 02

    Your hours, on paper

    A short written plan: every repetitive job we found, the hours it costs, and which one to automate first.

  3. Week 1-3 03

    Build

    We build the first automation against your real inbox, exports and systems. Not a sandbox.

  4. Week 3-4 04

    Run it side by side

    It runs next to your current process until you trust the output. Then the manual version stops.

  5. Day 30 05

    Live

    The first job runs on its own. Your team gets those hours back, starting that week.

  6. Every month 06

    Run and add

    We watch it, fix what breaks, and add the next job when you're ready. Cancel any time.

What you get

Clear terms. You own the result.

  • A fixed quote per build. You know the cost before anything starts.
  • Your systems, not new ones. We plug into the email, spreadsheets and software you already use.
  • A human in the loop. Drafts and payments wait for approval until you decide otherwise.
  • Plain-English documentation. What it does, where it runs, how to turn it off.
  • You own everything. The automations, the data and the docs are yours.
  • Month to month. Ongoing support with no long contract.

Questions

The ones everyone asks.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can tell us what your team does on a Monday morning, that is enough. We handle the rest and explain it in plain English.

Is my data safe?

We use the access each job needs and nothing more, keep humans approving anything that sends money or messages, and document exactly where your data goes. We will walk through it on the audit call.

What if it gets something wrong?

That is why every build runs side by side with your current process first, and why exceptions get flagged to a person instead of guessed at. When something breaks, we fix it as part of the monthly support.

Will this replace my staff?

It replaces the busywork, not the people. The goal is to give the team you already have their week back for the work that actually needs them.

How much does it cost?

Each build gets a fixed quote after the free audit, sized to the hours it saves. If the numbers don't make sense for you, we'll tell you.

How does pricing work?

Two parts. A one-time fixed price for each build, quoted after the audit. Then optional monthly support that covers monitoring, fixes and small changes, month to month.

What size business is this for?

Small businesses, usually somewhere between a few people and a few locations, where the owner or office manager is still doing a lot of the paperwork themselves. See who it's a good fit for.

Which AI do you use?

Whichever fits the job. Usually Claude or ChatGPT through business API access, where your data is not used to train the models. We don't resell software, so there is no reason to push one over another.

Will it work with the software we already have?

Almost always. If it has email, an export, a spreadsheet or an API, we can work with it. We check every system on the audit call before quoting.

What do you need from us?

About an hour for the audit, access to the systems the first job touches, and someone to check the output during the side-by-side run. After that, very little.

What happens if we stop working together?

You keep everything. The automations run in your accounts, the documentation explains how they work, and we hand over cleanly. Nothing is held hostage.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, before the audit if you'd like. Everything you show us stays confidential.

Do you work outside Southern California?

Yes. Audits and builds run over video and screen share, so we can work with businesses anywhere in the US.
