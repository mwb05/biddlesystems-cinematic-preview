How it works

Weeks, not quarters.

One task at a time, each proven before the next begins.

Day 30

From first call to hours back.

  1. Day 1 01

    Free audit call

  2. Day 2-5 02

    Your hours, on paper

    A short written plan listing each repetitive task, the time it costs, and the best fit: we build it, your team learns it, or Archer handles it.

  3. Week 1-3 03

    Build

    We build the first automation inside your actual inbox, exports and systems.

  4. Week 3-4 04

    Run it side by side

  5. Day 30 05

  6. Every month 06

Questions

The ones everyone asks.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can describe what your team does on a typical Monday morning, we can take it from there.

Which of the three should I start with?

If you want the work off your plate, start with automation services. If you would rather build the capability in-house, start with the courses or Archer. The audit recommends the right fit for each task.

Is my data safe?

Each job gets only the access it needs, a person approves anything that sends money or messages, and we document where your data goes.

Will this replace my staff?

No. It takes over the repetitive work so your people can spend their time on the parts of the job that need them.

How does pricing work?

A fixed price per build, quoted after the audit and sized to the hours it saves. Optional month-to-month support after that. If the numbers don't work for you, we'll say so.
