Accessibility

Built for everyone to use.

Biddle Systems wants every visitor to be able to read, navigate and contact us on this site, whatever tools they use. This page says what we do, where we fall short, and how to tell us when something does not work.

Last reviewed: October 22, 2026

Our standard

We aim for biddlesystems.com to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, the standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) guidance from the US Department of Justice and by Section 508. We also follow the newer WCAG 2.2 criteria where they apply, such as focus appearance and target size.

What we do

  • Keyboard. Every link, button, menu, form field, slider and expandable question works with the keyboard alone, in a sensible order, with a visible focus ring. A "Skip to content" link is the first thing the Tab key reaches on every page.
  • Screen readers. Pages use real headings, landmarks (header, navigation, main, footer), lists and labelled forms. Decorative panels, charts and animated illustrations are hidden from assistive technology, and the real numbers they illustrate are in the page text.
  • Contrast. Body text, headings, buttons and links meet at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against their background; large text and interface borders meet at least 3:1.
  • Text size and reflow. Text can be enlarged to 200% in the browser without loss, and the layout reflows to a single column at narrow widths (down to 320 pixels) with no sideways scrolling.
  • Forms. Every field has a visible label, required fields are announced, errors are reported next to the form, and the confirmation after sending is announced to screen readers.
  • No traps or time limits. Nothing on the site times out, auto-plays audio or flashes.
  • Windows High Contrast. Buttons, cards and fields keep visible borders when forced-colors mode is on.
  • Privacy. The site has no cookies or trackers, and fonts are served from our own site, so assistive technology does not have to contend with consent pop-ups.

Motion and animation

The site uses scroll-driven animation, a rotating globe illustration, moving text bands and animated counters. All of them stop when your operating system's "reduce motion" setting is on. If you have not set that, or want to turn animation off on just this site, press "Pause animations" in the footer of any page. The choice is remembered on your device and can be undone with "Resume animations".

With animation paused, every page shows its finished state: counters show their final numbers, the before-and-after comparison is laid out in full, and the process steps are shown as a plain list.

Known limitations

  • Savings calculator sliders. The sliders are native range inputs and work with the arrow keys, but adjusting a value precisely by keyboard takes several presses. Each slider's current value is read out as you change it. If the sliders are hard for you to use, email us the hours and we will run the numbers for you.
  • Illustrative panels. The sample report, inbox and invoice graphics on the Services and Home pages are decorative and are hidden from screen readers; their content is explained in the adjacent text.
  • Third-party pages. The contact form is delivered through Web3Forms. If the form cannot be sent, the page falls back to a standard submission; you can always email us directly instead.

How we check

This site is checked with automated accessibility testing (axe-core) on every page at desktop and phone widths, with keyboard-only navigation, with the "reduce motion" setting on and off, and by review of the HTML against WCAG 2.1 AA. The last review was on the date at the top of this page. Automated tools catch only part of the picture, so we rely on feedback for the rest.

Compatibility

The site is built with standard HTML, CSS and JavaScript and works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on desktop and mobile. It is designed to work with screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver, with browser zoom, and with the operating system's high-contrast and reduced-motion settings. It also works with JavaScript turned off: all content and the contact form remain usable.

Report a problem

If any part of this site is hard to use, or you need any of its content in another format, email support@biddlesystems.com with "Accessibility" in the subject. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or assistive technology you use. We reply within two business days and fix confirmed problems as a priority.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can also contact the US Department of Justice ADA Information Line at 800-514-0301 (voice) or 1-833-610-1264 (TTY), or visit ada.gov.
